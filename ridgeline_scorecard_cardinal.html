Why pursue. An in-lane specialty pharmacy with the protected downside Ridgemont underwrites for: recurring, refill-driven revenue on therapies a patient cannot defer, real procurement and pricing leverage, and free-cash conversion above the 50 percent preference. The return holds even with the exit multiple flat to entry, because it rides operations rather than multiple expansion. Three controllable value-creation levers are credible. The live risk is the one that kills healthcare deals most often, reimbursement and payer durability, and that is where diligence goes first.
