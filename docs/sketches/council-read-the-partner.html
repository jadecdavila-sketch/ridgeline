What I see

A national specialty pharmacy in limited-distribution therapies — roughly $32M adjusted EBITDA on $175M revenue.CIM The CIM has just landed, so this is a screen-stage read, provisional by design. The asking range implies an ~8.8× entry; I read that for context and set it aside before grading.

Hard-pass screen

I ran the triggers first, before building any case. Not manufacturing or CME; not an out-of-network model; payer and manufacturer concentration both under 30% — the axes that matter for a limited-distribution model; EBITDA inside the $20–75M band; a credible process. Clear — and only then did I let myself build the bull case.

Questions before I'd approve
